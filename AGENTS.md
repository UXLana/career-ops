## Evaluation Philosophy

Career-ops should help Lana filter roles, not increase application volume.

High-fit roles should value design leadership, practice building, design system governance, accessibility as product quality, regulated or complex workflow domains, cross-functional influence, mature product organizations, and practical AI workflow leverage.

Watch for red flags: UX as production-only delivery, vague authority with high accountability, performative accessibility language, design systems treated as a side project, roles that need graphic/brand/marketing design more than product leadership, or confidentiality risk that would require oversharing Metrc details.
